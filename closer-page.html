Before you choose

How does the leverage/transfer-over piece work?
As people in your customer base take daily action, some of them generate sales at levels you don't personally own — those commissions transfer to the closest member above them who does own that level. Higher positioning (LK3) is built to catch more of that transfer activity. Full mechanics are covered in member training.
Can I start at LK1 and move up later?
Yes. Your commission position is tied to the highest level you currently own, so moving up sooner means you're positioned to catch more of the transfer-over activity as it happens, rather than after the fact.
Refunds?
These are instant-access digital products. All sales are final — there's no refund window, so choose the tier that matches what you're ready to commit to.
